# Game Distribution Info

## Title
BlockOps: Team Deathmatch (or Blocky Shooter: Combat Arena)

## Short Description
Dive into fast-paced, action-packed 3D blocky combat! Join a team, equip your favorite weapon skin, and dominate the arena in this thrilling First-Person Shooter.

## Long Description
Welcome to the ultimate blocky combat experience! **BlockOps: Team Deathmatch** is an adrenaline-fueled 3D FPS where you fight alongside your team to claim victory. Jump into the arena as part of the Blue or Red team and battle against AI opponents in intense, time-limited matches.

Show off your style by unlocking and equipping various weapon skins (Default, Gold, Crimson, Neon, and Diamond). Master your aim, utilize cover, and secure the highest kill count to be crowned the Match MVP!

Whether you are playing on a desktop with a mouse and keyboard or on a mobile device with intuitive touch controls, the fast-paced gameplay and satisfying gunplay will keep you coming back for more.

## Key Features
- **Intense Team Deathmatch:** 5v5 (Player + AI Bots) fast-paced arena combat.
- **Weapon Skins:** Unlock and equip 5 unique gun skins (Default, Gold, Crimson, Neon, Diamond) using in-game currency earned by getting kills.
- **Cross-Platform Controls:** Seamlessly play on Desktop (Mouse + WASD) or Mobile (Touch Joysticks).
- **Match MVP:** At the end of every match, the best performing player is highlighted on the MVP screen with their character and stats.
- **Dynamic 3D Graphics:** Charming blocky/voxel-style visuals built on modern web technology (Three.js).
- **Immersive Audio:** Features satisfying sound effects for shooting, reloading, taking damage, and walking.

## How to Play (Instructions)

**Desktop Controls:**
- **W, A, S, D:** Move your character.
- **Mouse Movement:** Look around / Aim.
- **Left Mouse Click:** Shoot.
- **Spacebar:** Jump.
- **R:** Reload weapon.
- **Shift:** Sprint.

**Mobile Controls:**
- **Left Joystick:** Move your character.
- **Right Joystick / Screen Drag:** Look around / Aim.
- **Fire Button:** Shoot.
- **Jump Button:** Jump.
- **Reload Button:** Reload weapon.

## Tips for Success
- Aim for the head to deal maximum damage!
- Keep moving to avoid enemy fire.
- Keep an eye on your ammo and reload when you are behind cover.
- Stick with your AI teammates to overwhelm the enemy.
